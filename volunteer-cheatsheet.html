Pledge Ledger — Volunteer Quick Guide

Scan a completed pledge card → check it → save. About 15 seconds per card.

1. Open the app link the coordinator shared with you

Open it on your phone's browser. Go to the ⚙ Setup tab and type your name once, then tap Save settings. You only do this once — the app remembers it.

Scan a card

  1. Tap 📷 IntakeThen tap the big “Take / choose photo” box — the camera opens.
  2. Photograph the cardLay it flat, good light, whole card in the frame. Take the photo.
  3. Tap “✨ Read card with AI”Wait a few seconds while it reads the handwriting.
  4. Check the detailsFix anything wrong. Orange ⚠️ marks fields the AI wasn’t sure about.
  5. Tap “💾 Save to Google Sheet”You’ll see “Saved ✓ Donor #…”. That donor number is assigned for you.
  6. Next cardThe form clears itself — just start again from Intake.

Good photo

✔ Flat
✔ Bright
✔ All corners
✗ Angled
✗ Shadow
✗ Cut off

Tips

  • Leave Donor No blank — the app numbers it automatically.
  • Always glance over the fields before saving — the AI is good, not perfect.
  • Names/handwriting that’s been rewritten may misread — just correct it.

If something’s off

  • “unauthorized” → reopen the coordinator’s link (don’t type the address by hand).
  • Bad read → tap Retake and shoot again in better light.
  • Never type card numbers — the credit-card box is ignored on purpose.
